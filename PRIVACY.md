# Privacy Policy — YaCatala

**Last updated: October 14, 2026**

YaCatala is built by Xiangyu Sun (sole developer). It is a reading app for young children, played together with a parent, so this policy is short on purpose: **YaCatala collects no data.** Everything it stores stays on your device.

---

## What YaCatala stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

| Data | Why | Leaves the device? |
|------|-----|--------------------|
| Which words were played, answered and learned | To choose the next words and show progress on the 3×4 grid | No |
| Missions and practice days | The weekly Missió del so and the calendar | No |
| Sound pages and pictures you add | Pages made from a suggested page, a worksheet photo or Image Playground | No |
| Word recordings you make | Played back in the games instead of the synthetic voice | No |
| Settings (age, letter case, language choices) | Remembered between launches | No |

The app has no accounts, no login and no profiles with names: the two players are simply "the child" and "the grown-up".

## Camera, photos and microphone

All three are optional and are only used from the **parent area**, which sits behind an adult gate.

- **Camera** — to photograph a page of your child's school worksheet. The photo is processed on your device to cut out the pictures and words; only the cut-out pictures you keep are saved.
- **Photos** — to choose a worksheet photo you already have. The app only sees the photo you pick.
- **Microphone** — to record how a word is pronounced. Recording only happens between your taps on the record and stop buttons, and the recording stays on your device.

## Apple Intelligence and speech

- **Image Playground** (on devices with Apple Intelligence) draws missing pictures. It is Apple's system feature and is subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- Words are spoken with the speech voices built into iOS and iPadOS, on your device.

## Exporting progress

The parent area can export the learning log as a file, **only when you choose to**, through the standard share sheet. The file contains the Catalan words, answers and dates — no names, no photos and no recordings. Where it goes is up to you.

---

## What YaCatala does NOT do

- No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
- No ads, ever
- No data sent to any server operated by the developer
- No selling or sharing of data
- No accounts, no social features, no chat
- No in-app purchases at this time

---

## Children

YaCatala is made for children of around 5 to 7, playing with a parent. Because the app collects no personal information at all, it collects none from children. Anything that touches the camera, photos, microphone or sharing is behind an adult gate.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Inside the app, a parent can also start a sound over, which clears its progress, from the parent area.
